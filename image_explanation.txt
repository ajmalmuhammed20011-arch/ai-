The NASA EPIC image shows the visible disk of Earth against black space. The detector separates the disk from the background, then labels pixels as approximate ocean, cloud, land, smoke or haze, and coastal or hazy water. In the annotated result, yellow outlines mark large cloud regions and a red box marks a region classified as a possible smoke plume over Canada. The segmentation is based on colour rules and a manually selected region, so these labels are approximate visual estimates rather than verified scientific measurements. The script regenerates this explanation with the measured coverage percentages when it runs.
